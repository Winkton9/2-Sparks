About 2 Sparks Electrical

2 Sparks Electrical is a trusted, reliable electrical business serving homes and businesses across Poole, Bournemouth and the wider Dorset area. We take pride in high-quality workmanship, honest advice, and a friendly, down to earth approach that keeps customers coming back and recommending us to others.

The business was founded by Jason Bridges in 2017, after he decided to venture out on his own following years working in full time employment. Jason started his electrical journey back in 2001 and now has over twenty years of experience, covering everything from simple accessory changes and installs to full rewires and fault finding.

We offer a complete range of electrical services, from consumer unit upgrades and full rewires to EICRs, testing, fault finding and ongoing maintenance. Every job is tailored to suit the property and the budget, with clear communication, neat work, and results that last.

We are proud to serve our local areas of Poole, Bournemouth, Ferndown, Wimborne, Christchurch and beyond. Every job we take on is handled with care, precision and attention to detail, just as if it were our own home.

Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What services do you offer at 2 Sparks Electrical?

We provide a wide range of electrical services, including consumer unit upgrades, EICRs (Electrical Installation Condition Reports), installation work up to full rewires, and general electrical maintenance. Whether it is a small domestic job or a larger commercial project, we have the expertise to meet your needs.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Poole and cover Bournemouth, Ferndown, Wimborne, Christchurch and the wider Dorset area. If you are not sure whether you are within our patch, just give us a call on 07795 803729 and we will happily let you know.

Are you a qualified and certified electrician?

Yes. 2 Sparks Electrical is an NICEIC approved contractor, which means our work is regularly assessed against national safety standards. All installation and inspection work is carried out to the current wiring regulations and certified on completion.

How long have you been trading?

Jason founded 2 Sparks Electrical in 2017, and started his electrical career back in 2001, so there are over twenty years of experience behind the business. Jason attends jobs personally, so you get an experienced electrician rather than being passed to a subcontractor.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all projects, so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins. Call us on 07795 803729 or send an enquiry through the website and we will get back to you.

What are your working hours?

Our standard service hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. We can arrange Saturday visits by appointment for pre-scheduled work, and we do our best to help with genuine electrical emergencies outside those hours.

How long does it take to upgrade my consumer unit?

A consumer unit upgrade typically takes between four and six hours, depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the installation. We make sure your new unit provides better protection, improved safety, and full compliance with current electrical regulations.

How much does a new consumer unit cost?

Consumer unit upgrades typically start at around £1,000 plus VAT, though the exact price depends on your existing setup and the size of the property. We always provide a free, no-obligation quote so you know the full cost before we start.

Why should I upgrade my fuse board?

Older fuse boards often lack the RCD protection that modern consumer units provide, which guards against electric shock and reduces fire risk. An upgrade also gives each circuit its own protection, so a single fault no longer trips the whole house. It is one of the most worthwhile safety improvements you can make.

Will the work be certified?

Yes. Every consumer unit we install is fully tested and certified, so you have written proof the work meets current standards. This is useful for your own peace of mind, and for insurers or future buyers.

Can you add surge protection?

Yes. We can fit surge protection as part of your consumer unit upgrade, which helps protect sensitive appliances and electronics from voltage spikes on the supply. We will talk you through whether it is worthwhile for your property.

How do I know if I need an EICR?

An EICR is recommended for all properties, especially if they are rented out or over ten years old. It is also a legal requirement for landlords to ensure tenants are living in a safe environment. We carry out thorough testing and provide detailed reports, including certificates and advice on any repairs or upgrades needed.

What is included in an EICR?

An EICR checks the condition of your wiring, consumer unit, sockets and fittings against the current standards. We identify anything unsafe or non-compliant, grade it clearly, and provide a full written report with certification and honest advice on what needs attention.

Do landlords legally need an EICR?

Yes. Landlords in England are required to have the electrical installation in rented properties inspected and tested at least every five years, and to provide a report to tenants. We carry out landlord EICRs and issue the certificate you need to stay compliant.

How long does an EICR take?

Most domestic EICRs take a few hours, depending on the size of the property and the number of circuits. We will give you a clear idea of timing when you book, and we always aim to work around you with minimal disruption.

Can you fix any problems found during the EICR?

Yes. If the inspection uncovers faults or non-compliant work, we can quote to put it right and carry out the remedial work for you. We explain everything in plain English first, so you can decide what to do with no pressure.

Do you carry out full house rewires?

Yes. We handle both full and partial rewires, as well as smaller installation jobs. An older property with deteriorating wiring can be a real safety risk, and a rewire brings it fully up to date. We plan the work around you and keep disruption to a minimum.

Is any job too small?

Not at all. We are happy to help with everything from adding a single socket or light to wiring a kitchen or extension. Whatever the size of the job, it gets the same careful planning and neat, certified finish.

How much does a rewire cost?

Installation and rewire costs vary a lot depending on the size of the property and the scope of the work, so we quote each job individually. We provide a free, no-obligation quote after assessing the job, with a clear breakdown and no hidden extras.

Can you install outdoor sockets and lighting?

Yes. We install outdoor power and lighting, including garden lighting, security lights and sockets for sheds, garages and workshops, all wired to the correct standard for outdoor use.

Do you provide certificates for installation work?

Yes. All of our installation and rewire work is tested and certified on completion, giving you documented proof that the work is safe and compliant with the current wiring regulations.

What does your electrical maintenance cover?

Our maintenance service covers repairs, replacing faulty fittings and faceplates, smart technology installs, and general upkeep for homes and businesses. Whether it is a quick fix or ongoing support, we keep your electrics safe, efficient and up to date.

How much do you charge for maintenance work?

Electrical maintenance is charged at £85 plus VAT for the first hour, then £65 plus VAT for each hour after that, plus any materials. We always talk you through what is needed before starting, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with landlords and businesses?

Yes. We look after homeowners, landlords, property managers and businesses across Poole and Dorset. Regular maintenance helps prevent issues before they become costly problems, and we can work around your schedule where needed.

Can you install smart home technology?

Yes. We install smart thermostats, smart lighting, video doorbells and other connected devices, wiring them in properly so they work reliably. Just let us know what you would like and we will advise on the best setup.

Can you find and fix electrical faults?

Yes. Fault finding is one of our specialities. If a circuit keeps tripping or has stopped working, we track down the cause methodically and put it right, rather than just treating the symptom.
